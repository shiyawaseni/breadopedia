**Chinchirorin** (ちんちろりん), also known as the Dice Game (ダイスゲーム; _Daisu Geemu_), is a minigame in Suikoden, Suikoden II and Suikoden IV.
# Rules
Before the start of the game, players make a wager before the game starts.

The aim of the game is to roll three dice, get two dice to land on the same number and the remaining number will be that player's 'score'. This score is compared to the score of the other player and the highest scoring individual wins.

If both players end up with the same score, or fail to make any score at all, it's called a draw and neither one of them wins. The money has to be wagered anew for another game.
## Special Rules
If a player gets three of the same number (2, 3, 4, 5, or 6), they will automatically win the game and will win thrice the amount originally wagered. However, if a player gets three ones, they will automatically lose and must pay triple the amount wagered to their opponent. These two special arrangements are both called Storm.

Moreover, rolling 4-5-6 will automatically win, granting double the amount wagered. On the contrary, rolling a 1-2-3 is an automatic loss, and the roller must pay double the wager to their opponent.

| Roll  | Results | Multiplication |
| ----- | ------- | -------------: |
| 1-1-1 | Lose    |            -3× |
| 2-2-2 | Win     |             3× |
| 3-3-3 | Win     |             3× |
| 4-4-4 | Win     |             3× |
| 5-5-5 | Win     |             3× |
| 1-2-3 | Lose    |            -2× |
| 4-5-6 | Win     |             2× |
